Autorun examples

Curated hosted LMScript examples. Each script is fetched and run locally by lmctl autorun.

  • lmdraw_solo — Seed one lmdraw Lead to make a small drawing and leave its terminal ready.
  • lmdraw_trio — Seed a Lead, Coder, and Reviewer to make and assess a small drawing.
  • lmcad_solo — Seed one lmcad Lead to create and verify a small CAD model.
  • lmcad_trio — Seed a Lead, Coder, and Reviewer to create and assess a CAD model.
  • lmr_solo — Seed one lmr Lead to analyze a small data set and verify the result.
  • lmr_trio — Seed a Lead, Coder, and Reviewer to analyze a data set and verify the result.
  • lmhtml_solo — Seed one lmhtml Lead to audit a small HTML site and verify the report.
  • lmhtml_trio — Seed an lmhtml Lead, Coder, and Reviewer to audit and review an HTML task.
  • agy — Send a short greeting through Antigravity.
  • claude — Send a short greeting through Claude Code.
  • codex — Send a short greeting through Codex.
  • kimi — Send a short greeting through Kimi Code.
