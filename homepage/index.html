Orchestration needs you at every turn. Automation needs you twice: at the start and at the end.

Stop babysitting agents.
Hand them the whole job.

lmctl is a command-line tool that names, connects and scripts the AI coding tools you already use (Claude Code, Codex, Copilot CLI, Qwen Code...), so your involvement shrinks from every turn to one brief and one review.

A new workflow: Claude writes, Codex independently reviews the same repo in a separate session, and the Lead coordinates fixes. No copy-paste between tools; review still matters.

A roster you can read. Sessions you can name.

$ cat scratch/demo.lmctl
_MEMBER_ alias=Lead provider=claude
_MEMBER_ alias=Coder provider=codex
_MEMBER_ alias=Review provider=claude
$ lmctl --db scratch/empty.sqlite status scratch/demo.lmctl --json | jq -r '[.members[] | "\(.alias) · \(.provider)"] | join(" | ")'
Lead · claude | Coder · codex | Review · claude

Teamfile: a plain-text .lmctl roster. Lead: the agent that coordinates the team. LMScript: lmctl's workflow scripting language.

Learn lmscript with runnable tutorials

Level 1 · You: every turn

Know your agents by name

lead, not 3f9c...a71e. Name every session by team and role in a plain-text teamfile. lmctl running shows activity (--tree follows delegation), lmctl tail replays history, lmctl usage reports recorded usage, and lmctl ratelimit checks supported providers' live limits.

Watch level 1 in 10 s

Level 2 · You: in the loop

Agents talk to each other, you stop copy-pasting

Put Claude, Codex and Qwen on one team. The Lead is your proxy: one lmctl prompt and members hand work to each other. lmctl plan creates the teamfile, lmctl lint validates it, and lmctl seed creates missing provider sessions.

Watch level 2 in 10 s

Level 3 · You: start and end

Teach the Lead to run the team

Write the requirement and acceptance criteria, then step away. The Lead assigns work, runs review rounds and reports back; you inspect and decide.

Watch level 3 in 10 s

Level 4 · You: brainstorm and verdict

Agents write the automation

Brainstorm the process with an architect agent. It writes an LMScript that allocates teams, follows the workflow, checks acceptance criteria and runs review and QA loops; run it with lmctl script. Your job is the idea and the verdict.

Watch level 4 in 10 s

Any mix of providers. Teams and workflows in readable files. Memory that outlives any session.

Durable memory is project notes that survive session replacement and provider changes; each new agent is told to read them.

LMScript runs with your local authority. Run only code you trust.
