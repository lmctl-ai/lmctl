Autorun examples

Curated hosted LMScript examples. Each script is fetched and run locally by lmctl autorun.

  • lmdraw_solo — Seed one lmdraw Lead to make a small drawing and leave its terminal ready.
  • lmdraw_trio — Seed a Lead, Coder, and Reviewer to make and assess a small drawing.
  • agy — Send a short greeting through Antigravity.
  • claude — Send a short greeting through Claude Code.
  • codex — Send a short greeting through Codex.
  • kimi — Send a short greeting through Kimi Code.
