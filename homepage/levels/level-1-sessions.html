Level 1 · Agent session management

Give AI sessions names and a team

Organize provider sessions by team and member name, then inspect activity, history, usage and provider limits.

Video description: Three labeled rows gather under Lead, Coder and Review teams. A lens zooms in on one session as a running indicator and usage bar appear.

The idea

A plain-text .lmctl teamfile gives each provider session a team and member name, so you can see who is doing what without relying on UUIDs. The same teamfile can hold sessions from different supported provider tools.

What ships today

lmctl status lists the configured team; lmctl running shows active work; lmctl tail peeks at session history; lmctl usage summarizes recorded usage; and lmctl ratelimit reports provider limits.
