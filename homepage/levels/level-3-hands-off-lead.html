Level 3 · Human-defined goal and review

Let a Lead handle the middle

Set the request and acceptance criteria, delegate the middle steps to a Lead, and review the returned result.

Video description: A requirement and acceptance checklist enter from the left. The Lead coordinates the work in the middle, and the result returns to the right for review.

The idea

At this level, a person writes the requirement and acceptance criteria before work starts. A Lead assigns tasks and coordinates its members while the person steps away from individual turns; the result returns for human review.

What ships today

Use lmctl prompt to start the request and lmctl status or lmctl tail to monitor the team and its run. lmctl records prompts and runs, but it does not decide which approvals are required. Put workflow boundaries in the team instructions, then inspect the result against the acceptance criteria before accepting it.
